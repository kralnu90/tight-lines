# Tight Lines

**A survival fishing adventure for your phone, by Kralnu Games.**

Fish the rivers, lakes and coast of a busy, trading world. You'll read the conditions, fight every catch the way real anglers do, and get home to the tavern before the cold, hunger or the dark gets you.

▶ **Play the prototype:** https://kralnugames.com/tight-lines/

**Current version: 0.28.0** · experimental build

> Tight Lines is in an early, experimental phase. Expect bugs, glitches and changes between updates, and saves may occasionally need to be cleared.

## What's in the game so far

- **Real fishing fights:** cast with a timing swing, wait out the nibbles, strike when the float goes under, then play the fish. Hold to reel, move the rod with or against its runs, and watch the line tension. Too much and it snaps; too little and the hook slips.
- **Three fishing spots and four towns:** Willowmere River, Willowmere Lake and the sea at Saltmarsh Point, plus the riverside village of Willowmere, the lakeside village of Thornmere, the seaside village of Saltwick and the city of Kingsbridge. A region map shows them all with travel times.
- **34 fish**, each fighting its own way: 7 river, 9 lake, 7 coast, 4 boat and 7 deep-sea species, from roach and mackerel to pike, conger eel, tope, halibut and bluefin tuna.
- **Four fishing methods** that play differently: float, ledger, spinning and fly.
- **Conditions matter:** season, time of day, weather, method, bait and line weight all change what bites. The odds are shown on screen.
- **Unpredictable bites:** the float dips, shakes, bobs and slides, some fish fake it, and takes can plunge, slide away or lift the float flat. Fly fish splash at your fly and miss.
- **Weather:** clear, overcast, rain, wind and fog. Wind drifts your float and gusts during the fight; fog brings out the predators.
- **Fish journal:** a page per species with your records and every condition you've caught it in.
- **Traps and pots** from fishing level 20: crayfish, brown crab and lobster.
- **Survival:** food, warmth, energy, health and water, on a live day and night clock with 10-day seasons.
- **Injuries:** open wounds, sprained ankles, cracked ribs and being chilled to the bone. They last until they heal, and some need treating. Bandages help, and a room at the tavern dresses everything.
- **Camp:** light a fire, cook your catch, boil river water, rest, or sleep until dawn. Camping never saves.
- **The Willowmere Arms:** the tavern is the only place to save. A night there restores you and refills your water.
- **Four difficulty levels:** Easy is pure fishing with no needs to manage, Medium is the game as designed, Hard toughens the fish and the wild, and Extreme is brutal and always hardcore. Harder levels turn up more rare fish and give more XP.
- **Normal or Hardcore:** die on Normal and you wake at your last tavern save. On Hardcore, death is permanent and your angler goes on the tavern's memory board.
- **Fish Bucks and trade:** sell your catch at the inn in either town. Size, stars and freshness set the price. Willowmere pays more for lake fish, Thornmere for sea fish and Saltwick for river fish, and each pays less for its own kind. Kingsbridge, the city, pays a fair price for everything and top money for rare fish and dishes. Regional dishes sell for more away from home, and each town's market drops if you flood it.
- **Tackle shop and graded gear:** buy bait, lures, line, rods, reels and kit. Gear comes in Basic, Good, Fine and Master grades, and the shop's gear stock changes daily. Rods, reels and line all change how a fight plays.
- **Events:** things happen while you travel, rest and sleep out. Wolves, wild boar, bears, a fox thief, a stranger at the fire, an abandoned camp and more. Pick a choice, roll a d20 and add your bonuses: skill level, a campfire, a storm lantern after dark, and penalties for being hurt or exhausted.
- **Hunting:** set snares and check back after a few hours, or go tracking for rabbits, game birds, roe deer, wild boar and the red stag. A hunting bow gives +5 from hunting level 10.
- **Foraging:** herbs, berries, wild veg, mushrooms, coastal finds, spices, rare herbs and truffles, depending on your level, the season and where you are. Dig your own bait from foraging level 5.
- **Larder:** game and foraged food spoil over time. Eat it, cook it, or brew rare herbs into teas. Only fish products sell.
- **Cooking:** every fish starts with a gutting and filleting dice roll. Cook and eat it, cook it to sell, salt it, smoke it, or cook one of 13 recipes with foraged ingredients. Learn recipes from Maggie, the cook at the Willowmere Arms, from strangers and from abandoned camps. Cooked, preserved and recipe fish all sell for more than raw.
- **Chum:** spoiled fish isn't wasted. Chop it up, throw it in, and bites come much quicker for two hours.
- **Skills:** fishing, hunting, foraging and cooking each level from 1 to 50, with unlocks every 5 levels. Cooking your catch at camp trains cooking, and events train hunting and foraging.
- **Three save slots, a log book with star ratings and personal bests, and fishing XP.**
- **Request boards and reputation:** every inn has a board of orders that changes every two days: fresh catches, specimen fish, top-quality fish, rare fish, salted or smoked fish, recipe dishes and parcels to deliver to other towns. Take on three at a time and beat the deadline for Fish Bucks and sometimes a recipe, gear or rare tackle. Each town remembers you: good standing means better prices, better orders and more in the shop, and missed deadlines cost you.
- **News, gossip and festivals:** prices in other towns are hidden until you hear about them. Read the Kingsbridge Courier or buy a round at the bar for market news, festival dates and old anglers' tips on where rare fish turn up. Storms, gluts, banquets and weddings shift prices for a few days, and five festivals through the year pay big for certain fish and dishes. Seasonal fish sell for less while everyone's catching them and more out of season.
- **Storage:** your keep net holds 12 slots and bigger fish take more (salted and smoked fish pack smaller). A big keep net adds 8. Rent a locker at any inn for 10 FB a season, but what you leave there stays in that town.
- **Vehicles and travel:** every trip is a short drive scene that matches the time, weather and where you're going (tap to skip, or turn it off in Settings). Your old estate car's boot adds 6 slots. Kingsbridge Motors sells a cool box (fresh fish keep half as long again), a fishing van (16 slots) and a campervan with a bed, heater, stove and fridge: sleep warm and safe wherever you park.
- **Boats:** sail from Saltwick harbour. Hire Jory's boat from fishing level 25 for six hours in Saltwick Bay (thornback ray, black bream, and more tope, cod and conger), and from level 30 out to the Marguerite wreck (ling, ballan wrasse, conger). Buy your own cobble from level 35.
- **Deep sea:** from fishing level 40, Jory (or your own offshore cruiser) takes you out to the Kettle Deeps. Fights there have a depth meter: pump and wind to haul fish up from 40 to 70 m while they dive back down. Haddock, hake, monkfish and turbot, then big game from level 45: blue shark, Atlantic halibut and bluefin tuna.
- **Online leaderboard:** see the heaviest fish of every species landed by other players. Your angler's best catch of each species posts automatically when you're online, and waits on your phone until you are. No account or code needed, and you can turn sharing off in Settings.

## How to play

| Action | Touch | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cast | Drag down on the water, release at the end of the swing. Or, with motion casting on: hold Cast, swing the phone back, flick forward and let go | Mouse drag |
| Aim the cast | Drag sideways while swinging back | Mouse drag |
| Strike (float, ledger, fly) | Tap **Strike** when the float goes under, the rod tip pulls round or a fish rises | Space |
| Retrieve (spinning) | Hold **Hold to retrieve** | Space or W |
| Reel | Hold **Reel** | Space or W |
| Move the rod | Drag left and right on the river | A / D or arrow keys |
| Net the fish | Tap **Net** when it's close and tired | N |
| Menu | Tap **Menu** | Esc |

**Tips**
- When a fish bolts away from you, stop reeling and move the rod with it until it slows.
- Move the rod *with* a running fish to ease the tension. Move it *against* the run to tire the fish faster, but tension rises.
- Chub, tench, pollock and conger bolt for the snags, weed or rocks. Turn them away fast.
- When a trout or salmon jumps, stop reeling.
- Keep the line tight when an eel starts rolling, or it'll tangle.
- Don't spend a night in the open without a fire.
- Can't afford a room? The stable is free and still saves, but it's a rough night.

## Install it as an app

On Android (Chrome), the game offers to install itself when you open it, or you can use **Install the app** on the main menu. On iPhone or iPad, open it in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Once installed it runs full screen and works without a signal.

## Notes

- The game is the single `index.html` file. `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js` and the `icons` folder make it installable and playable offline. No accounts needed.
- The online leaderboard is the only part of the game that uses the internet. It shares your angler name and each species' heaviest catch, nothing else. Everything else still works offline.
- Saves are kept in your browser on each device, so clearing your browser data clears your saves.
- Built phone-first, but it plays in a desktop browser too.

## Updates

The game checks for a newer version when it opens and when you come back to it. If there is one, players see a small "A new version is ready" notice with an **Update** button, and there's a **Get the latest version** button in the menu at any time.

When pushing an update: change the game's version number and put the same value in `version.json`.
