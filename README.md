# Tight Lines

**A survival fishing adventure for your phone, by Kralnu Games.**

Fish the rivers, lakes and coast of a busy, trading world. You'll read the conditions, fight every catch the way real anglers do, and get home to the tavern before the cold, hunger or the dark gets you.

▶ **Play the prototype:** https://kralnu90.github.io/tight-lines/

> This is an early prototype. It's playable, but a lot is still to come (see the roadmap below).

---

## What's in this version

- **Real fishing fights:** cast with a timing swing, wait out the nibbles, strike when the float goes under, then play the fish. Hold to reel, move the rod with or against its runs, and watch the line tension. Too much and it snaps; too little and the hook slips.
- **Seven river fish**, each fighting its own way: dace, chub, brown trout, grayling, European eel, barbel and Atlantic salmon.
- **Conditions matter:** season, time of day, weather, bait and line weight all change what bites. The odds are shown on screen.
- **Survival:** food, warmth, energy, health and water, on a live day and night clock with 10-day seasons.
- **Camp:** light a fire, cook your catch, boil river water, rest, or sleep until dawn. Camping never saves.
- **The Willowmere Arms:** the tavern is the only place to save. A night there restores you and refills your water.
- **Normal or Hardcore:** die on Normal and you wake at your last tavern save. On Hardcore, death is permanent and your angler goes on the tavern's memory board.
- **Three save slots, a log book with star ratings and personal bests, and fishing XP.**

## How to play

| Action | Touch | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cast | Drag down on the water, release at the end of the swing | Mouse drag |
| Aim the cast | Drag sideways while swinging back | Mouse drag |
| Strike | Tap **Strike** when the float goes under | Space |
| Reel | Hold **Reel** | Space or W |
| Move the rod | Drag left and right on the river | A / D or arrow keys |
| Net the fish | Tap **Net** when it's close and tired | N |

**Tips**
- Move the rod *with* a running fish to ease the tension. Move it *against* the run to tire the fish faster, but tension rises.
- Chub bolt for the snags under the willow. Turn them away fast.
- When a trout or salmon jumps, stop reeling.
- Keep the line tight when an eel starts rolling, or it'll tangle.
- Don't spend a night in the open without a fire.

## Notes

- The whole game is the single `index.html` file. No installs or accounts needed.
- Saves are kept in your browser on each device, so clearing your browser data clears your saves.
- Built phone-first, but it plays in a desktop browser too.
