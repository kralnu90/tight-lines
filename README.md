# Tight Lines

**A survival fishing adventure for your phone, by Kralnu Games.**

Fish the rivers, lakes and coast of a busy, trading world. You'll read the conditions, fight every catch the way real anglers do, and get home to the tavern before the cold, hunger or the dark gets you.

▶ **Play the prototype:** https://kralnugames.com/tight-lines/

**Current version: 0.12.0** · experimental build

> Tight Lines is in an early, experimental phase. Expect bugs, glitches and changes between updates, and saves may occasionally need to be cleared.

---

## What's in the game so far

- **Real fishing fights:** cast with a timing swing, wait out the nibbles, strike when the float goes under, then play the fish. Hold to reel, move the rod with or against its runs, and watch the line tension. Too much and it snaps; too little and the hook slips.
- **Three fishing spots:** Willowmere River, Willowmere Lake and the sea at Saltmarsh Point, each with its own snags to avoid.
- **23 fish**, each fighting its own way: 7 river, 9 lake and 7 coast species, from roach and mackerel to pike, carp, conger eel and tope.
- **Four fishing methods** that play differently: float, ledger, spinning and fly.
- **Conditions matter:** season, time of day, weather, method, bait and line weight all change what bites. The odds are shown on screen.
- **Survival:** food, warmth, energy, health and water, on a live day and night clock with 10-day seasons.
- **Camp:** light a fire, cook your catch, boil river water, rest, or sleep until dawn. Camping never saves.
- **The Willowmere Arms:** the tavern is the only place to save. A night there restores you and refills your water.
- **Four difficulty levels:** Easy is pure fishing with no needs to manage, Medium is the game as designed, Hard toughens the fish and the wild, and Extreme is brutal and always hardcore. Harder levels turn up more rare fish and give more XP.
- **Normal or Hardcore:** die on Normal and you wake at your last tavern save. On Hardcore, death is permanent and your angler goes on the tavern's memory board.
- **Fish Bucks:** sell your catch at the Willowmere Arms. Size, stars and freshness set the price, lake fish pay more in riverside Willowmere, and flooding the market drops prices for a while.
- **Tackle shop and graded gear:** buy bait, lures, line, rods, reels and kit. Gear comes in Basic, Good, Fine and Master grades, and the shop's gear stock changes daily. Rods, reels and line all change how a fight plays.
- **Three save slots, a log book with star ratings and personal bests, and fishing XP.**
- **Online leaderboard:** see the heaviest fish of every species landed by other players. Your angler's best catch of each species posts automatically when you're online, and waits on your phone until you are. No account or code needed, and you can turn sharing off in Settings.

## How to play

| Action | Touch | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cast | Drag down on the water, release at the end of the swing | Mouse drag |
| Aim the cast | Drag sideways while swinging back | Mouse drag |
| Strike (float, ledger, fly) | Tap **Strike** when the float goes under, the rod tip pulls round or a fish rises | Space |
| Retrieve (spinning) | Hold **Hold to retrieve** | Space or W |
| Reel | Hold **Reel** | Space or W |
| Move the rod | Drag left and right on the river | A / D or arrow keys |
| Net the fish | Tap **Net** when it's close and tired | N |
| Menu | Tap **Menu** | Esc |

**Tips**
- Move the rod *with* a running fish to ease the tension. Move it *against* the run to tire the fish faster, but tension rises.
- Chub, tench, pollock and conger bolt for the snags, weed or rocks. Turn them away fast.
- When a trout or salmon jumps, stop reeling.
- Keep the line tight when an eel starts rolling, or it'll tangle.
- Don't spend a night in the open without a fire.
- Can't afford a room? The stable is free and still saves, but it's a rough night.

## Install it as an app

On Android (Chrome), the game offers to install itself when you open it, or you can use **Install the app** on the main menu. On iPhone or iPad, open it in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Once installed it runs full screen and works without a signal.

## Notes

- The game is the single `index.html` file. `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js` and the `icons` folder make it installable and playable offline. No accounts needed.
- The online leaderboard is the only part of the game that uses the internet. It shares your angler name and each species' heaviest catch, nothing else. Everything else still works offline.
- Saves are kept in your browser on each device, so clearing your browser data clears your saves.
- Built phone-first, but it plays in a desktop browser too.
